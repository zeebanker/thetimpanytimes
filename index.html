In Loving Memory

Remembering Jazz.

Dr. Mercy Jeyaraja Rao

July 4, 1930 — March 22, 2026

Known to generations of Timpots simply as Jazz, Dr. Rao served Timpany School for forty-eight years, from 1954 to 2002 — rising from teacher to Headmistress, Principal, Executive Director, and General Director.

Born in Visakhapatnam on July 4, 1930, she led the school through its transformation into a high school and its 1964 move to Waltair. Beyond Timpany, she served globally as President of the Asian Baptist Women's Union and in the Baptist World Alliance.

"A strict yet God-fearing, loving, amazing woman."

In her care, thousands of Timpots were shaped into who they became. We gather here her stories, letters, photographs, and the lessons she left behind.

After years of scattered WhatsApp messages, reunion photos that never quite reach everyone, and memories shared in small circles, we decided it was time to bring the Timpany community together in one place.

The Timpany Times is a bimonthly newsletter — written, edited, and designed entirely by former students of Timpany School. It belongs to no institution. It answers to no administration. It belongs to us.

Every two months, we’ll bring you stories from the corridors we walked, updates from classmates near and far, and news of the community we share. Here’s what’s coming in future issues:

The teachers, the friendships, the moments that stayed.
Featured Articles
Profiles, reflections, spotlights on prominent alumni, and essays from Timpots around the world.
Class Notes & Notables

Life Events

Events Calendar
Reunions, meetups, and gatherings across cities.
